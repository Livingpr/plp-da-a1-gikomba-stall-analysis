# Gikomba Market Stall Analysis

**Author:** EUNICE LIVINGPROOF | **Cohort:** 1 MILDEVS

## Problem

Wanjiku runs a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market and has recorded 14 days of sales and a rough stock register. She wants to know how the business is doing, whether it is growing, what to restock and which days to focus on. This analysis uses only core Python (no pandas or NumPy) to answer those questions.

## How to run

[![Open In Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/assets/colab-badge.svg)](https://colab.research.google.com/github/Livingpr/plp-da-a1-gikomba-stall-analysis/blob/main/stall_analysis.ipynb)

Open `stall_analysis.ipynb` in Google Colab and select Runtime, then Run all.

## Top three findings

1. **Weekends drive sales.** Weekend days average KES 4,900 against KES 2,685 on weekdays, which is 82.5% higher.
2. **The business is growing.** Week 2 sales were KES 24,400 against KES 22,050 in week 1, up 10.66%, for a 14-day total of KES 46,450.
3. **Two products need restocking now.** Tomatoes (18 kg against a reorder level of 25 kg) and sukuma (9 kg against 15 kg) are below their reorder levels.
